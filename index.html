Why We Exist

We make objects that are not finished when they leave the workshop.

A piece of solid teak keeps changing long after it reaches your home — the grain deepens, the surface catches light differently with each year of use. We build for that slow change, not against it.

This is a small family workshop in India, run by the same hands for three generations. We make fewer things, more carefully, and we are in no hurry to make more.

Craftsmanship

Every piece passes through one pair of hands, start to finish.

We work in solid timber, never veneer or particleboard. A single block is selected, dried, and left to settle before a tool ever touches it — rushing this step is the most common way a piece fails early.

Joints are cut by hand and fitted dry before any finish is applied. The oil we use is food-safe and reapplied over the piece's life, not sealed under lacquer that traps moisture and cracks.

The Workshop

A family workshop, unchanged in method for three generations.

The tools are old. The hands are patient. Nothing here is made to a deadline — a piece is finished when it is ready, and not before.
